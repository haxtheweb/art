The best costume is one you can wear all night without tripping, squinting, or disappearing into the dark.

Be seen in the dark

Add reflective tape to costumes and treat bags, and carry a flashlight or a glow stick.

Masks block your view

Face paint is a safer choice than a mask that covers your eyes. If you wear a mask, make sure the eye holes are big enough to see curbs and cars.

Trip-proof your hem

Costumes should stop above the ankle, and shoes should fit. Capes are great. Capes on staircases are less great.
