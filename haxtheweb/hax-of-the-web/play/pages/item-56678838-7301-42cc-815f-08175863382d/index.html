Haunting the neighborhood is a team sport.

Use the buddy system

Young trick-or-treaters go with a trusted adult. Older kids stay in a group and share their route with someone at home.

Only visit lit houses

A porch light is the universal sign for "come get candy." Never go inside a stranger's home or car.

Walk, don't sprint

Use sidewalks, cross at corners, and look both ways. Drivers have a hard time seeing small witches at dusk.
