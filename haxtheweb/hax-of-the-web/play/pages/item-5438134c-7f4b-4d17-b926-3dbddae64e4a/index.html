Welcome, ghouls and goblins! This little site is your guide to a Halloween that is fun, spooky, and safe for everyone in the house.

Your guide tonight is our zombie pirate girl. She has survived three hundred Halloweens, mostly by following the tips in this course.

What you will learn

  • Pick a costume you can see, walk, and be seen in
  • Trick-or-treat with a buddy and a plan
  • Check your candy before you eat it
  • Carve pumpkins without carving yourself
