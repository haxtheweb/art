HAX the Web

1 player · attract mode

Press Enter or tap the screen to play the intro jingle and start a run. Jump with Space, a tap, or the Jump button. Check Play slower for a gentler pace; the jumps and the world stay the same, everything just moves at 60% speed. The yellow arrows on the ground show where to jump, and each glowing badge you grab teaches one piece of HAX along the bottom of the screen. Your runner is a real rpg-character built from the player seed, so change it to meet someone new. Leave the title screen alone and a demo plays by itself.
