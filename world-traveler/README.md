# The World Traveler

![All 36 World Traveler variants](contact-sheet.jpg)

> **Dream it, remix it, have it, share it.**

The World Traveler (a.k.a. **World Changer**, **Future Traveler**) is the mascot of [HAX](https://haxtheweb.org). He is fun, a bit silly, and on a mission: to travel to the future and bring the rest of us along.

## Story

He moves through time, from exploring Egypt to 8-bit worlds, taking the best ideas and concepts of each era and carrying them forward so they can improve every space we work in. He has seen a future where **data flows like water**, and he is trying to transport it back so everyone can see what he sees as possible.

The point of HAX is the same point: unlock human creativity and potential by removing the need to understand the web in order to have a well-structured voice on it.

He is a variant of the **rpg-character**, itself a caricature of HAX's creator, who wears a goofy hat with steampunk goggles and "sees the future".

## Character sheet (what makes him on-model)

| Feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red helmet with a **HAX** visor band | He wears the project's name. |
| Steampunk goggles, usually cyan or green lenses | Sees the future. |
| **Lightbulb** on a stalk or cable | The idea he carries; creativity. |
| Green backpack | The ideas collected on his travels. |
| Chin on arms, peeking over a desk/edge in a starburst circle | The classic pose (see #15). |

Colors: red, deep green, cyan, warm yellow. Tone: playful, never snarky.

Variants change **style and setting** (pixel art, paper-cut, trading card, comic, 3D, cyberpunk, Egypt, Halloween), not the core features.

## Why so many styles?

Remixing is the point. The variants are inspired remixes of other works of art, much like HAX's building blocks and remixing capabilities. Many were generated with AI tools from the original human-made character (some carry a generator's watermark). HAX's own writing and brand marks are not AI-generated.

Because some variants parody other creators' work, they are marked `third_party_reference` in [`manifest.json`](../manifest.json) and should be reviewed before being used in official promotion. See [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md).

## Making new on-model variants

When prompting an image tool, include: *red helmet with a "HAX" visor band, steampunk goggles with cyan lenses, green backpack, lightbulb on a stalk, friendly and slightly silly*, then name the **style** and **setting** (e.g. "stained glass, medieval library"). Check the result against the character sheet, add it as the next numbered file, and add a manifest entry with truthful `alt` text and provenance.
