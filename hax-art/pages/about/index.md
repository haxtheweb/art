HAX art collects the art and media of the HAX project: the Future Traveler mascot, brand assets, posters and materials from talks, browsed with HAX itself.

How you may reuse the art is described in [LICENSING.md](https://github.com/haxtheweb/art/blob/main/LICENSING.md); some pieces reference other works, and that file says which.

It is made by the HAX project (HAXTheWeb), an open-source effort to let anyone author, remix and publish on the web without needing to learn how the web works. This site is itself a HAX site; [its source is on GitHub](https://github.com/haxtheweb/art).

To see how every part of HAX connects, visit [hax.cloud](https://hax.cloud/). To get in touch, use the [Contact](contact) page.
