RPG me is one of the tools of the HAX art movement: a character builder for the `rpg-character`. Pick a base, face, hair, clothes, skin, hat and more, and RPG me gives you a character code and a link that rebuild the character that stands for you across HAX, on any page that loads HAX.

RPG me is its own app, at its own address: rpg.art.hax.cloud. It lives here because it is part of the art; it runs on its own so you can share it, link to it and come back to it. Its old address, rpg-me.hax.cloud, takes you there.
