The `rpg-character` is a HAX web component: a little pixel character built from a seed, with hats, costumes, pets and more. Every piece here uses the element's own art. Here is one, live on this page:

*   [rpg-character on GitHub](https://github.com/haxtheweb/webcomponents/tree/main/elements/rpg-character)
*   [Make your own in rpg-me](https://rpg-me.hax.cloud/)
