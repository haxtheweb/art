RPG me is one of the tools of the HAX art movement: a character builder for the `rpg-character`. Pick a base, face, hair, clothes, skin, hat and more, and RPG me gives you a seed and a link for the character that stands for you across HAX. That character walks in the HAX network bar on every HAX site.

RPG me is its own app, at its own address. It lives here because it is part of the art; it runs on its own so you can share it, link to it and come back to it.
