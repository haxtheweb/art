The Future Traveler has collected the best ideas of every era in his backpack, so his icon can be drawn the way each era would have drawn it: an 1880s engraving, a 1920s gold line drawing, a neon sign, four greens on a handheld screen. He is still him in all of them: the helmet with HAX on it, the goggles, the lightbulb.

These are the eras the Future Traveler remixes through at the right end of the HAX network bar: hover him and he tears through every one, click and he stays in the next. Each follows one of the styles the [posters](posters) tell the story of the pillars in, and the world of tomorrow comes once per pillar, in that pillar's color.

The icons are drawn in code (SVG) by Claude, an AI, under HAX team direction.
