A space-opera opening crawl that catches people up on HAX: where it came from, the web standards it stands on, and where it is in 2026. It ends on a Join the Rebellion card. Viewers with reduced motion get a static, readable version.

A parody of the classic sci-fi film opening crawl. The text and the synthesized fanfare are original, and no footage, logo or score is used.
